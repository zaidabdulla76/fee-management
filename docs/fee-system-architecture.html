01

Overview

The system tracks Hijri-calendar student billing across two fundamentally different fee lifecycles that share one schema. Tuition is enrollment-driven: a student is enrolled once per academic year and the system generates 12 monthly bills up front, each of which can sit Pending, go Overdue, and accrue a late fee before it's paid. Books, Exam, Uniform, and Bag are collection-driven: nothing is generated in advance — Admin picks the fee type and a priced variant at the moment a student pays, and the bill is born already Paid.

Everything below is organized around that split, because it's the one design decision the rest of the architecture has to accommodate: one MonthlyBill table, two ways a row in it comes to exist.

The Two Bill-Creation Paths

This is the architectural fork everything else hangs off. FeeType.mode is either Enrollment (Tuition, and only Tuition) or Collection (Books, Exam, Uniform, Bag). The two modes don't just price differently — they create rows in MonthlyBill at different times, for different reasons, and one of them can go overdue while the other structurally cannot.

Snapshot, don't reference. MonthlyBill.amount is copied from ClassFee or FeeItem at the moment the bill is created, not looked up live. A price change in Settings only affects bills generated after the change — issued bills keep the amount they were born with.
05

Key Flows

5.1 Non-Tuition Collection (FR-12)

The defining property of this flow is that step 4 does two writes in one transaction — there's no intermediate state where a bill exists without a payment, or vice versa.

5.2 Late-Fee Overdue Sweep (FR-10)

The only writer that touches bills after they're created. Runs once nightly; scoped to Tuition by construction, since non-Tuition bills never carry a due date to compare against.

Deployment Topology

08

Non-Functional Requirements → Mechanism

NFRConcrete mechanism
NFR-1 Data integrityDB grants: Payment table is INSERT-only for the API's role — no UPDATE/DELETE grant exists. The one sanctioned bill mutation (late-fee stamping) is scoped to two columns via a dedicated internal endpoint, not general write access.
NFR-2 Localeamount columns are numeric(10,2), rendered with a fixed ₹ prefix. Dates stored as Gregorian date; Hijri month is a label join via HijriMonth, never a computed conversion.
NFR-3 AuthenticationJWT bearer token, short-lived access token, checked by FastAPI dependency on every route except /auth/login and the scheduler's internal route (network-restricted instead).
NFR-4 Auditabilitycreated_by, created_at on Payment from day one, even with a single shared admin login — groundwork for the future per-user audit log without a schema change later.
NFR-5 ScaleIndexes on MonthlyBill(student_id, academic_year_id) and (status, due_date) for the sweep query. Low-thousands of students × ~14 bills/year keeps the whole working set comfortably in memory.
09

Open Architecture Questions

Sweep frequency

Nightly cron assumed (00:15). If admins need same-day Overdue flags for bills due that morning, the sweep may need to run more than once a day, or overdue status may need to be computed lazily on read instead of stamped by a job.

Retiring a Fee Item

Marking a book or uniform size active = false hides it from the FR-12 dropdown going forward. Because bill amounts are snapshotted (Section 4), this is safe to do mid-year without touching history — but needs a "why is this greyed out" affordance in the UI.

Export library choice

WeasyPrint (PDF) and openpyxl (Excel) assumed as the lowest-friction fit for a Python backend. Neither is a hard requirement — swappable if the report layouts turn out to need something heavier.

Concurrent collection

Two admins recording the same student's Uniform payment seconds apart isn't prevented by anything beyond normal transaction isolation — worth a duplicate-detection check (same student, fee item, day) before this ships, or it's an accepted risk given a single-admin-login reality.
